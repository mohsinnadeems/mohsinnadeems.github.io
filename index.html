Responsible AI

In regulated industries a model is only useful if it can be trusted, explained and audited. I build governance into the machine learning lifecycle from the start, aligned to three frameworks.

ISO/IEC 42001AI management systems

Risk and impact assessments for each model, clear accountability, and controls across the AI lifecycle.

ISO/IEC 27001Information security

Access control, secure pipelines and protected training data, so ML systems meet the same security bar as the rest of the business.

GDPRData protection

Lawful, minimal use of personal data, with privacy considered from design through to model retirement.

How it shows up in my work

  1. DesignDefine the model's purpose, risks and impact on people before any build starts.
  2. DataDocument data sources, lineage and quality checks, and confirm data protection requirements are met.
  3. BuildFavour explainable approaches, test for bias and robustness, and record every decision.
  4. DeployVersion models and experiments in MLflow, so every result can be traced and reproduced.
  5. MonitorTrack performance and drift in production, with clear triggers for review or retirement.

The result is audit-ready documentation that lets compliance teams and regulators see how a model works and why it can be trusted.
